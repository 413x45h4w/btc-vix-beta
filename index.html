Definitions and sources

Beta = Cov(BTC log return %, ΔVIX pts) / Var(ΔVIX pts): the slope of a regression of BTC % moves on VIX point changes. A beta of −1.0 means BTC has typically fallen 1% for each 1-point rise in VIX. Corr is the correlation of the same two series.

Hourly: completed 60-minute VIX bars during the US cash session (bar ends ≈ 10:30, 11:30 … 15:30, close). Overnight gaps are excluded. BTC is sampled from 15-minute Coinbase candles at each bar end. Daily: VIX close vs BTC at the last full hour before the VIX close (≈16:00 ET).

VIX: Yahoo Finance (^VIX, may be delayed). BTC: Coinbase Exchange BTC-USD. The repo's GitHub Action refreshes this every hour; raw series are in data/hourly.csv and data/daily.csv.
